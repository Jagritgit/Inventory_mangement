ROLE:
Act as a senior Django full-stack developer. Implement a Bill system that is created from Purchase Orders and displays data in a real-world bill format.

---

SYSTEM CONTEXT:

* Django Inventory Management System
* Existing models:

  * Vendor
  * Product
  * PurchaseOrder
  * PurchaseOrderItem

RELATION:

* PurchaseOrder → Vendor
* PurchaseOrder → PurchaseOrderItems (products, quantity, price)

---

## CORE REQUIREMENT

Bills must be created FROM Purchase Orders, not manually from scratch.

---

## 1. BILL MODEL

Create Bill model:

Fields:

* id
* vendor (ForeignKey → Vendor)
* purchase_order (ForeignKey → PurchaseOrder)
* bill_date
* total_amount
* status:

  * unpaid
  * paid
* created_at

---

Create BillItem model:

Fields:

* bill (FK)
* product (FK)
* quantity
* cost_price
* total_price (quantity × cost_price)

---

## 2. CREATE BILL FROM PURCHASE ORDER

Implement functionality:

* Button: "Create Bill" on Purchase Order page

When clicked:

1. Fetch PurchaseOrder

2. Create Bill:

   * vendor = purchase_order.vendor
   * purchase_order = selected order
   * total_amount = calculated

3. For each PurchaseOrderItem:
   → Create BillItem:
   - product
   - quantity
   - cost_price = price_per_item
   - total_price

---

## 3. COPY DATA FROM PURCHASE ORDER

Bill must include:

* Vendor details
* Product names
* Quantities
* Cost price per item
* Total amount
* Delivery/received status (from PurchaseOrder)

---

## 4. UPDATE PRODUCT DATA (IMPORTANT)

When Bill is created:

* Update product:

  * cost_price = latest bill price

* Optional:

  * Update stock if order is marked "received"

---

## 5. REAL-WORLD BILL DESIGN (IMPORTANT)

Bill must look like real printed bills:

### Structure:

1. Header:

   * Company Name
   * Address
   * Contact info

2. Bill Info:

   * Bill Number
   * Date
   * Vendor Name

3. Vendor Details:

   * Company Name
   * Phone
   * Address

4. Product Table:
   Columns:

   * Product Name
   * Quantity
   * Price per item
   * Total

5. Summary:

   * Subtotal
   * Total Amount

6. Status:

   * Paid / Unpaid

7. Delivery Info:

   * Purchase Order status (received or pending)

---

## 6. UI REQUIREMENTS

Purchase Order Page:

* Button: "Create Bill"

Bill Page:

* Clean printable layout (A4 friendly)
* No fancy UI
* Use table for product list

---

## 7. CONSTRAINTS

* Do NOT create bill manually without PurchaseOrder

* Do NOT duplicate product data incorrectly

* Keep relations clean:
  PurchaseOrder → Bill → BillItems

* Do NOT add unnecessary UI elements

---

## 8. OUTPUT FORMAT

Provide:

1. models.py (Bill, BillItem)
2. views.py (create bill from purchase order)
3. urls.py
4. template (real-world bill layout)
5. short explanation

---

## EXPECTED RESULT

* Bill is created from Purchase Order with one click
* All data is copied correctly
* Bill shows vendor, products, quantity, price, total
* Bill looks like a real printed document
* Product cost price updates correctly

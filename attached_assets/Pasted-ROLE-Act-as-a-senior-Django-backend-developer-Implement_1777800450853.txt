## 2. PURCHASE ORDER MODULE

Purpose:
Track what products you ordered from vendors and whether they are received

---

### PurchaseOrder model:

Fields:

* id
* vendor (ForeignKey → Vendor)
* order_date
* status:

  * pending
  * received
* total_amount (calculated)

---

### PurchaseOrderItem model:

Fields:

* purchase_order (FK)
* product (FK)
* quantity
* price_per_item
* total_price (quantity × price)

---

## 3. PURCHASE ORDER FEATURES

### Create Purchase Order:

* Select Vendor

* Add multiple products

* Enter:

  * quantity
  * price per item

* Auto-calculate:
  total_price per item
  total_amount of order

---

### Status Tracking:

* Default: pending
* When items received:
  → status = "received"

---

## 4. UI REQUIREMENTS

## 5. IMPORTANT LOGIC

* total_price = quantity × price_per_item
